Send AgentiSend's email events to Novu so they appear in the Activity Feed.

<Note>
  Email Activity Tracking is available on **Novu Cloud** and **Enterprise self-hosted** only. It is not included in the **Community self-hosted** edition. Contact us at sales@novu.co to learn more about Enterprise self-hosted licensing.
</Note>

## Step 1: Get your webhook URL from Novu

1. Navigate to the **Integration Store** page in Novu and select your **AgentiSend** integration.
2. Enable the **Email Activity Tracking** toggle.
3. A unique Inbound Webhook URL will be displayed. Click the **copy** icon to copy it.

## Step 2: Create the webhook in AgentiSend

1. In the AgentiSend console, open **Webhooks** (or call `POST /webhooks`).
2. Create an endpoint with:
    - **URL:** the **Inbound Webhook URL** you copied from Novu.
    - **Events:** `email.sent`, `email.delivered`, `email.delivery_delayed`, `email.bounced`, `email.complained`, `email.suppressed`, `email.failed`, `email.opened` and `email.clicked`.
    - **Payload format:** leave it as `native`.
3. Save the endpoint.

## Step 3: Add the signing secret (Recommended)

1. Copy the endpoint's **signing secret**. It is shown once, when the endpoint is created.
2. Return to your AgentiSend integration settings in Novu.
3. Under the **Email Activity Tracking** section, paste the secret into the **Inbound Webhook Signing Key** field.
4. Click **Save Changes**.

Novu then checks the `x-agentisend-signature` header on every delivery and refuses one older than five minutes.
